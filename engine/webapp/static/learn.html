Your Western star sign gives everyone born in the same month one label. Korean saju (사주, the "Four Pillars of Destiny") reads you from your exact birth date, time and place — as five living elements. Then it points to the one you're missing.

Saju vs. Western astrology

Western sun-sign astrology reads the position of the sun in the zodiac. Saju is a calendar-based system built from the Chinese-Korean sexagenary cycle and the 24 solar terms — so it uses your birth year, month, day and hour (eight characters, 八字) rather than a single sign. Because it's grounded in your exact moment of birth, it's far more specific than "you're a Gemini."

We call it "Korean astrology" as an umbrella term for discovery, but saju isn't star-reading — it's calendar and element reading, which is exactly why it can be more precise.

The five elements (오행)

Saju maps your eight characters onto five elements. Most people lean heavy on some and light on others.
